The final design contains 3985 total cells with a core area of approximately 33344.48 um^2. The synthesized netlist includes approximately 32 D flip-flops along with combinational arithmetic and control logic dominated by AOI/OAI-style gates, XOR/XNOR logic, multiplexers, and OR/NOR cells. These cells mainly support the signed multiply-accumulate datapath and registered accumulation logic used in the compute core.
